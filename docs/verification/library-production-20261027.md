# Unified knowledge library: production verification (sanitized)

This public record summarizes production verification of a unified knowledge library generation. It contains method and caveat information only; real library figures, receipt names, private paths, identifiers, and source details are omitted. Detailed measurements are retained in private evidence.

## What was verified

Independent acceptance checks confirmed, in aggregate, that a published generation combined authored material and AI work while retaining normalized revisions and source evidence. Verification covered:

- consistency of published records, observations, revisions, snapshots, and retained identities against source expectations,
- reconciliation of previously affected records and supplemental document identities,
- integrity checks for captured original files where applicable,
- agreement between reading index/CSV exports and current database identities,
- validation of report/pointer lineage, artifact inventory, hashes, and selected archive member contents as part of independent audit steps.

Logical object integrity and complete Markdown-link checks were treated as build-verifier responsibilities where not fully repeated by the independent audit.

## Coverage and limitations

- Full source coverage was not complete; partial and metadata-only records remained visible by design.
- Encrypted content was not presented as decoded text, and recovered binary text with unknown roles remained partial.
- Successful library publication did not establish Notion import success or native Obsidian acceptance.
- Search/retrieval checks exercised sampled pages and bounded reads; they did not claim exhaustive manual inspection of every result.
- Reading notes may normalize display or replace repeated prose with links, while original text remains available through raw revision reads. Historical source pointers do not guarantee the original file still exists at its source location.

## Reproduction evidence

Reproduction evidence and aggregate acceptance artifacts are retained in the owner's private store. Public source material includes reader regression tests and bounded build, resume, export, and publication tests.
